# Choreless — Clip Factory Launch Kit (Week 1)

**Goal:** 5–10 free clips delivered → screenshots → first paying customer.
**Rule:** Don't sell. Deliver something good for free, in public, and let people ask you.

---

## The offer (one line, use everywhere)
> "Send me a long video, I'll turn it into 3 captioned shorts + thumbnails, free. First 5 people. I score each clip for retention so you post the best one."

---

## 1. Reddit — r/NewTubers (post this first)

**Title:** I'll edit 3 shorts from your long video for free — first 5 people (I want portfolio proof)

**Body:**
> I'm building a tool that turns long videos into captioned shorts and I need real examples to prove it works. So I'm doing the first 5 for free, no catch.
>
> Send me a link to one long video (podcast, vlog, tutorial — anything 10min+). You get back:
> - 3 vertical shorts, captioned and cut for hooks
> - Thumbnails for each
> - A quick note on which one I'd post first and why
>
> Drop your video in the comments or DM me. I'll take the first 5. If it's useful, I'll tell you what it'd cost going forward — but the free ones are genuinely free.

*(Post the same in r/youtubers a day later. Read each sub's self-promo rules first — lead with the free value, not the product.)*

---

## 2. X / Twitter — build in public (post as you deliver)

**Kickoff post:**
> Starting something: I turn creators' long videos into scroll-stopping shorts.
>
> Doing the first 5 free to build proof.
>
> Reply with a video link 👇 I'll post the results here as I finish them.

**Delivery post (repeat for every clip — THIS is the engine):**
> Creator sent a 22-min podcast this morning.
> 3 shorts shipped by [time].
> Here's the best one 👇 [video]
> Retention score: [X]. Free clip #[N] of 5.

Every delivery post is an ad that cost you nothing. This is the whole growth loop.

---

## 3. Creator Discord — DM template

> Hey — saw you post long-form. I'm doing free short-form edits (3 clips + thumbnails) for the first few creators to build proof. Want me to do one of your recent videos? No cost, no strings.

Pick ONE Discord you're already in. Don't spam five.

---

## First-week plan (concierge MVP)
1. **Day 1:** Post to r/NewTubers + X kickoff.
2. **Days 1–4:** Deliver each request by hand (you + Claude). Speed matters more than perfection — 24h turnaround feels magic.
3. **Every delivery:** Screenshot the clip + the creator's reaction. Save to a `/receipts` folder.
4. **Day 5:** Post the X delivery threads. Ask the happy ones: "Would you pay $X/mo for this every week?"
5. **Day 7:** First paid offer to anyone who said yes → they become customer #1 and the first real testimonial on the site.

## What NOT to do
- Don't launch all 12 services. One wedge.
- Don't buy ads. Your proof is free and shareable.
- Don't promise what you can't deliver in 24h. Under-promise, over-deliver, screenshot it.
