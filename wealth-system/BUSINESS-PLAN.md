# Reputation Engine — the $100k system

> **The honest version first.** No software generates money on its own. This is a
> business you run. What makes it a "system" is that every part — who you sell to,

---

## 1. What the business is

**Reputation Engine** is a done-for-you Google-reviews and reputation service for
local businesses that live or die by their star rating: dentists, HVAC/plumbing,
med spas, law firms, auto shops, home services, clinics.

You sell one outcome: **"More 5-star reviews, every review answered, a report each
month — without them lifting a finger."**

### What you actually deliver each month
1. **Review-request automation.** After every job/appointment, the customer gets an
   SMS/email asking for a review, with a one-tap link to the client's Google profile.
   Happy customers post publicly; unhappy ones get routed to a private feedback form
   first (this is legitimate service recovery, not review gating of star ratings).
2. **AI-drafted responses to 100% of reviews** — you approve and post them. Fast,
   on-brand replies to good and bad reviews measurably lift conversion and ranking.
3. **A one-page monthly report** — new reviews, rating change, response rate. This
   single page is why they keep paying: they *see* the value.

### Why this works in 2026 (and isn't hype)
- ~90% of consumers read reviews before choosing a local business; a jump from 3.9
  to 4.5 stars measurably increases calls and bookings. The owner knows this and
  still doesn't do it — they're busy running the business.
- The tools to run it (SMS/review platforms + an AI model to draft replies) are cheap
  and mature. Your cost per client is tens of dollars; you charge hundreds.
- It's **recurring by nature** — reputation is never "done," so clients stay for years.
- It's **provable in the sale**: you can pull up their Google profile on the call and
  show them, live, that they have 12 reviews and the competitor down the street has
  340. The gap sells it.

## 3. Startup cost & risk (be honest with yourself)

**Cash to start: under $500.**
- Review/SMS platform (starts ~$50–100/mo) · AI API or a chat plan (~$20/mo) ·
  domain + business email (~$20/mo) · a simple landing page (free — one is in this repo).

**The real cost is your time for the first 90 days** doing outreach before revenue
ramps. This is the honest risk: if you won't do consistent outreach (calls, emails,
walk-ins) for 8–12 weeks, no plan works. The failure mode is *quitting outreach*, not
the model being wrong.

**Other real risks & mitigations**
- *"I can't get clients."* → Lead with a free audit (see playbook). Value is visible
  before they pay. Book 10 audits/week, close 2–3.
- *Churn.* → The monthly report is the retention product. If they can see the wins,
  they don't cancel. Target churn under 4%/mo.
- *Delivery overwhelm.* → It's automated by design. One person comfortably handles
  30–50 clients. Past that, hire a VA for reporting/posting.
- *Platform rules.* → Never fake reviews or gate star ratings — that gets clients
  penalized and is unethical. You automate *asking* real customers and *responding*.
  Legit and durable.

## 4. Who you sell to (pick ONE niche first)

Do not sell "local businesses." Pick **one** vertical, become the obvious choice, then
expand. Best starting niches (high review-sensitivity, real budgets):

1. **Dentists / orthodontists** — high customer value, review-obsessed, marketing budget.
2. **Med spas / aesthetics** — booming, image-driven, reviews = bookings.
3. **HVAC / plumbing / home services** — emergency-driven, competitive, reviews = calls.
4. **Law firms (personal injury, family)** — enormous case value, few reviews.

Pick the one you have any connection to. Sell 5–10 in that niche, use them as
identical case studies, and every next sale gets easier because you speak their language.

## 6. How you get clients (the only part that requires guts)

The channels that actually work for a local B2B service, in order:

1. **Free "Reputation Audit" as the wedge.** Make a 1-page PDF/loom for a specific
   business: their star rating, review count, response rate, vs. 2 competitors, and 3
   fixes. Send 10–20/day. It's personalized, undeniable, and leads straight to "want
   me to just handle this?"
2. **Cold email + SMS + calls** to a scraped list of your niche in your metro. 20
   contacts/day, 5 days/week = 400/month → realistically 8–12 audits → 2–4 clients.
3. **Walk-ins** (for home-service/retail niches) — physical presence converts.
4. **Referrals + partners** — once you have 5 happy clients, ask each for one intro,
   and partner with people who already sell to your niche (web designers, MSPs).

**The weekly scoreboard that gets you to $100k:** 100 outreach touches → 10 audits
booked → 2–3 new clients. Repeat every week. That's the entire growth engine.

## 7. Your first 90 days

See `90-DAY-PLAYBOOK.md` for the week-by-week version. Summary:

- **Weeks 1–2 — Set up.** Pick niche. Buy the stack. Build the delivery flow once
  (review-request sequence + AI reply prompt + report template). Land your landing page.
- **Weeks 3–6 — Sell.** Send the free audit daily. Book audits. Close your first 3–5 at
  a founder rate. Deliver flawlessly; collect the first win screenshots.
- **Weeks 7–12 — Systematize & scale.** Turn wins into case studies. Raise price to
  full. Push outreach to 100 touches/week. Target 10–15 clients by day 90.

By the end of 90 days you're at ~$3,500–5,250 MRR. From there it's repetition to 30.
