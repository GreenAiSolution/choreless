# 90-Day Playbook — zero to your first ~$5k/mo

The plan only works if you run it. This is the week-by-week. Do the daily numbers and
you land ~10–15 clients by day 90, which is ~$3,500–5,250 MRR and the on-ramp to $100k.

**The daily minimum that matters:** 20 outreach touches / weekday. That's it. Everything
else supports that one habit.

---

## Phase 1 — Set up the machine (Weeks 1–2)

**Goal: be able to deliver flawlessly for one client before you sell to anyone.**

- [ ] **Pick ONE niche** (dentists, med spas, HVAC, or law firms). Write down why.
- [ ] **Register** a name + domain + business email. Keep it plain and trustworthy.
- [ ] **Buy the stack:**
  - A review-management / SMS platform with review-request automation.
  - An AI plan or API key for drafting review responses.
  - A scheduling link (for booking audits/calls).
- [ ] **Build the delivery flow once:**
  - Review-request SMS/email sequence (post-appointment trigger).
  - A saved AI prompt that drafts on-brand replies to 5-star, 3-star, and 1-star reviews.
  - A one-page monthly report template (rating, new reviews, response rate, wins).
- [ ] **Put up a simple landing page.** A one-pager with the offer, 3 bullets, and a
      "Book a free audit" button is enough. (The Choreless site in this repo is a
      starting point you can strip down.)
- [ ] **Build your list:** 200+ businesses in your niche + metro, with owner name,
      phone, email, current Google rating + review count.

## Phase 2 — Sell your first 5 (Weeks 3–6)

**Goal: 3–5 paying clients at a founder rate. Value shown before money asked.**

- [ ] **Send the free Reputation Audit** — 15–20/day. One page: their stars/reviews vs.
      2 competitors + 3 fixes. Personalized = replied to. (Template in `SALES-ASSETS.md`.)
- [ ] **Book audits:** aim 8–12 booked calls over these 4 weeks.
- [ ] **Run the sales call** (script in `SALES-ASSETS.md`): show the gap, present the
      offer, ask for the free trial start. Don't over-explain — the audit already sold it.
- [ ] **Close 3–5** at a founder rate (e.g. $250/mo locked for being early).
- [ ] **Onboard + deliver** within 48h. Get the review-request flow live fast so they
      see new reviews arriving in week one. Screenshot every win.

## Phase 3 — Systematize & scale (Weeks 7–12)

**Goal: 10–15 clients, full price, repeatable outreach.**

- [ ] **Turn wins into 2–3 case studies:** "Went from 12 → 41 reviews, 4.1 → 4.7 stars
      in 6 weeks." One page each. These now do your selling.
- [ ] **Raise the price to full** ($350–$497) for all new clients.
- [ ] **Scale outreach to 100 touches/week.** Same audit wedge, now with case studies
      attached — close rate goes up.
- [ ] **Ask every happy client for one referral** and one Google review of *you*.
- [ ] **Track the scoreboard weekly** (below). Adjust the input that's lagging.

---

## The weekly scoreboard (the whole business on one line)

| Metric | Weekly target |
|---|---|
| Outreach touches (email/SMS/call) | 100 |
| Free audits sent | 40–60 |
| Audit calls booked | 8–12 |
| New clients closed | 2–3 |
| Clients lost (churn) | ≤ 1 |
| MRR added | +$700–1,050 |

If a week misses, look up the funnel: not enough touches → not enough audits → not
enough calls → not enough clients. Fix the earliest leak first.

---

## After day 90 (getting to $100k)

You're at ~10–15 clients. Now it's pure repetition:
- Keep closing 2–3 net/month. You cross ~30 clients around month 12–15.
- **Raise prices** on renewals as your case studies get stronger.
- **Add a second niche** only after the first is saturated or referrals dry up.
- **Hire a VA** (~month 20+, 25+ clients) for posting responses and building reports so
  you spend your time only on sales.
- Open `100k-roadmap.html`, put in your *real* current numbers, and read the crossover
  month. Then protect the two levers that move it most: **price** and **churn**.

The business keeps paying you long after $100k. That's the point of a recurring model.
