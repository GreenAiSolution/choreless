## 4. The sales-call script (15 minutes)

**1. Frame (30 sec).** "Thanks for the time. I'll keep this to 15 minutes. I pulled your
reputation numbers — want to walk through what I found and how we'd fix it?"

**2. Show the gap (3 min).** Share screen / the audit. State the facts, don't sell yet.
"You're at [4.1] with [12] reviews. [Competitor] is at [4.6] with [214]. When someone
searches '[dentist near me]', that's who they call."

**3. The cause (2 min).** "It's not that your customers are unhappy — it's that nobody's
asking them at the right moment, and no one's replying to reviews. Both are fixable and
neither should cost you any time."

**4. The offer (2 min).** "Here's what I do: after every appointment, your customer
automatically gets a text asking for a review with a one-tap link. Every review that
comes in, I draft a reply and post it. You get a one-page report each month. $350/mo,
cancel anytime — and the first month is a free trial so you can see the reviews start
coming in before you pay anything."

**5. Close (silence).** "Want me to get it set up this week?" — then stop talking.

**6. Onboard on the spot.** If yes: "Great — I need 3 things: access to your Google
profile, your booking/CRM so requests trigger automatically, and 5 minutes on your tone.
I'll have it live in 48 hours."

## 5. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We're too busy." | "That's exactly who it's for — it runs without you. The only thing I need is 5 minutes to start." |
| "We already ask for reviews." | "Most do, manually, and it's inconsistent — [12] in [3] years shows it. This makes it automatic after *every* visit." |
| "Can't I just do this myself?" | "You could — the same way you could do your own books. This is $350 to never think about it and get a report that proves it's working." |
| "Is buying reviews allowed?" | "We never buy or fake anything — that gets you penalized. We automate asking your *real* customers and replying. 100% legit." |
| "Too expensive." | "One new patient/job is worth what — [$500+]? A half-star bump brings in more than one a month. It pays for itself or you cancel." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Totally fair — that's why the first month's free. Start it, watch the reviews come in, decide then. Fair?" |

## 6. Onboarding checklist (deliver within 48h)

- [ ] Access: Google Business Profile (manager), booking/CRM for the trigger.
- [ ] 5-min tone call → save it into the AI reply prompt.
- [ ] Turn on the post-visit review-request sequence.
- [ ] Reply to their 3 most recent unanswered reviews (instant visible win).
- [ ] Send a "you're live" note + when the first report lands.
- [ ] Calendar reminder: monthly report on the 1st.

**First win fast = low churn.** Get a new review in week one and they never cancel.
