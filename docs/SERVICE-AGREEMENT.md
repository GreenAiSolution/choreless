## 5. What we promise — and don't

We operate the system with reasonable skill and care, monitor it, and fix
faults we control. The agents are AI: they are constrained to your documents
and gated on writes, but **no AI is infallible** — you review CONFIRM prompts
before approving, and we are not liable for actions you confirm, for content
of your documents, or for outages of third parties (Meta/WhatsApp, OpenAI,
MongoDB, Twilio, Stripe). Our total liability in any 12 months is capped at
the fees you paid in those 12 months. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential loss.

## 6. Fair use

Your subscription includes up to **{{3,000 / 8,000 / 20,000}} inbound
conversations per month**. If you exceed it two months running, we'll propose
a revised fee before anything changes — we never bill overage retroactively
or throttle you without a conversation first.

## 7. Data

Your knowledge base and conversation history are isolated to your business,
never used to train models, and never shared. On termination we delete them
within 30 days and confirm in writing. We retain operational logs (message
counts, action audit trail) as required to run and bill the service.

## 8. Term & exit

Month to month after go-live. Either party may end it with **30 days' written
notice**. The assembly fee is non-refundable once go-live is delivered; if we
fail to deliver go-live within 14 days of §3's conditions being met, you may
cancel for a full refund. Sections 5, 7 and 9 survive termination.
